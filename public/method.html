Fact Weight helps a reader compare a specific claim with specific documents. It does not decide whether a person is a bot, establish an author's intent, or treat an outlet as an absolute authority.

How a label is made

  1. You paste a post and choose up to four article URLs.
  2. Context.dev extracts their text. Fresh mode requests a new extraction. Retrieval time and any provider cache metadata stay in the evidence record.
  3. Hostinger AI Router extracts checkable claims and proposes passage relations.
  4. Code rejects quotations that do not occur verbatim in the extracted text. It groups articles from the same domain and near-identical extracted text.
  5. Accepted supporting and contradicting groups determine the label.

One source group is enough for a relation label. It is not enough to establish a fact. Group counts describe documents, not votes, and are never converted into a probability.

Where this can fail

A quotation match only proves that those words were in the extraction. The model can still misunderstand them. Separate domains may copy a single wire report, share ownership, or repeat the same official statement. The grouping heuristic cannot prove independence.

You choose the documents. An incomplete or biased source selection gives an incomplete view. A page can be stale, blocked, mistranslated, wrong or missing context. Retrieval time is not publication time. This version does not search the whole web or run a continuous feed.

Our synthetic evaluation uses constructed passages and labels. It does not establish real-world disinformation precision, human agreement, multilingual accuracy or calibrated confidence. Those require an independently labelled source corpus and review.

Privacy and limits

Your post and extracted passages go to Hostinger AI Router. Article URLs go to Context.dev. The application does not write your post to disk. It holds up to 200 extracted pages in process memory for five minutes of reuse, and remembers the previous extracted text hash until cache eviction or restart. Provider data handling applies. Avoid private or personal information.

The demo limits each connection to four requests per minute, two concurrent analyses, four sources per request and 150 analyses per process per day. The counter resets on a process restart, so it is a demo abuse limit rather than a durable billing cap.
