Know who is influencing your country, and what they want.

Signal Fire reads your country’s biggest stories of the week, finds every near-identical copy on the open web, and groups the sites that copy together within minutes. Nobody is on a list in advance. The copying gives them away.

Copied story

The copying gives them away

Real outlets write their own words on their own schedule. Coordinated sites publish the same text within minutes, story after story. That rhythm is the evidence.

  1. Read the week’s top stories

    About 40 stories per country from the open web, in the country’s own languages and English.

  2. Search for copies

    Distinctive sentences from each story are searched across the web. A copy must match at least 60% of the wording.

  3. Group sites that copy together

    Sites that copy three or more of the same stories within 60 minutes of each other form a group.

  4. Show who and what

    Domain ages, shared hosting and code, cited sources, and the lines they add that mainstream coverage does not have.
