# Hostinger: Decode the Noise

Signal Fire monitors the documented Pravda publishing network across the Baltics and Finland. It finds stories repeated across country sites, traces their cited origins, and looks for the same claims in public conversation. A fixed rule measures copied wording, shared links, timing and available account evidence. The Hostinger AI Router checks narrative agreement and weighs checkable claims against selected reporting and trackers. Every shown judgement records its model, prompt version and time.

The product shows its reasons, exact source passages, public links and an exportable evidence pack. Missing account histories remain unknown. A person can dispute a verdict without changing the evidence. The watch runs in an isolated container on a Hostinger VPS. Signal Fire flags coordination and weak evidence, whoever is behind it. It never reports or removes posts.

# Context.dev: The Web Is Your Real-Time Database

The Web, Watched. Signal Fire turns changes on public war-news pages and channels into an inspectable investigation. Context.dev retrieves country-site copies, Telegram previews, social-platform records and trusted reporting. Signed monitor webhooks notify the VPS when watched pages or searches change. The receiver verifies and deduplicates each event before queueing collection, grouping, measured signals and an evidence pack.

The app shows the actual requests, per-step times and observed first-post delays. Method separates live processing from historical collection and reports measured call times, feature usage and credits. Parsers, scraping, search, structured extraction and batch collection each have a recorded role. Public pickup is distinguished from a proven copying connection. Users can start a private watch for another conflict or known network. The service runs on Hostinger and continues with this Mac off.
