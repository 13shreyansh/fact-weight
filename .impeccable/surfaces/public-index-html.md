# Signal Fire site (landing, country, story X-ray, method)

Scope: landing is Persuade; country, story X-ray and method pages are Operate/Read. Audience: analysts, journalists, citizens, hackathon judges trying any country live. Job: pick a country, see who is copying its news in coordinated bursts, what they push, and the evidence per story. Constraints: real API data only, verdict words fixed, X not covered, fast static HTML/CSS/JS.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every copy carries a postmark. The site treats a story as a letter and each copy as a forwarded item stamped with site, country and minutes-after-original. It refuses the dark threat-intel dashboard with a glowing globe.

OWN-WORLD: Postal yellow (#FFCC00 family) owns whole fields; black ink; white label stock for evidence; airmail blue for mainstream/original; cancellation red only for the coordinated verdict. Barlow Condensed display set in heavy caps like sorting-office signage, Barlow body, JetBrains Mono only for timestamps, lags and IDs. Components: registered-mail labels (ruled boxes, 2px black rules, routing codes), circular postmark cancellations with wavy cancel lines, perforated edges on story slips.

STORY: Visitor learns influence is visible through copying, believes it from one real story stamped across countries within minutes, picks their country.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 55%: headline "Know who is influencing your country, and what they want." in black condensed caps on yellow, one-line mechanism, country search field plus ready-country chips. Right 45%: a white label slip of the most-copied real story with postmarks dropping in arrival order and the tally (copies, countries, minutes).

FORM: Postal/registered-mail system, candidate 6 of 7, seed key 7c3b9f59 (degraded roll, code-led). Signature interaction: postmark replay on story X-ray and hero.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
